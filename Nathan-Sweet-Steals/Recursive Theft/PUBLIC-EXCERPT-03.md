## Historical text

> Recursive Constraint Falsification is a disciplined way to keep what Segall gets right about theory-ladenness while refusing the upgrade to evidential exemption.

> It treats every claim about reality as a bundle: a target claim plus auxiliary assumptions plus measurement choices plus language that compresses a messy interface into a sentence.

> The method does not pretend we can escape interpretation. It forces interpretation to show its commitments, pay its bills, and accept revision.
